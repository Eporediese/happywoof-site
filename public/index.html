Nature Hikes for Happy Dogs

We bring exercise, companionship, joy, and an exciting break into the daily routine of dogs in the Georgetown, Connecticut area.

Since 2007, the Happy Woof pack has grown into a community of dogs that are comfortable with each other and themselves. Our four-legged friends love jumping into the Woof Mobile together for their next adventure.

Every hike is supervised, safe, and designed to let dogs be dogs — exploring trails, making friends, and coming home happily tired.

Old Fellows Always Welcome

Age is just a number in the Happy Woof pack. Our senior dogs bring wisdom, calm energy, and gentle companionship to every adventure.

Whether they prefer a leisurely stroll or still have pep in their step, every old fellow gets the pace and care they deserve.

Dogs Will Be Challenged to Their Best Abilities on Our Walks

Whether staying calm and obedient on the leash, or being left alone to explore amongst our safe pack of friendly dogs—each pup discovers their own boundaries!
